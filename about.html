Our Story

Braai Nation Shisanyama started in 2021 in Meadowlands, Soweto. We wanted to bring people together over fire-grilled meat, good music, and community vibes. What started as weekend braai stand is now Soweto's home for legendary wors and steak.


Our Mission

To serve authontic South African shisanyama with big flavor and even bigger portions


Our Vision

To be number 1 in Gauteng for food, music, and good times.
